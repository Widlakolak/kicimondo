# Originality and naming

Kicimondo is the chosen working title in both languages. Kicipedia is the name of the in-game cat collection. Kicikademia and other location names remain provisional.

The project draws on broad mechanics common in top-down adventure games, habitat-building games and cat collection games. Implement an original world, UI, stories, characters, art and music. Do not reproduce a distinctive map, screen layout, sprite, character, dialogue, sound effect, logo or source code from another game. Do not use names derived from existing franchise-specific terms or imply affiliation. In particular, do not use names ending in "-dex" for the collection.

Prefer original art and independently licensed assets. Check each asset's source, attribution, redistribution and modification conditions; free download does not mean free commercial use. Record them in ASSET_LICENSES.md. Do not copy web search thumbnails or game screenshots as assets.

No formal trademark clearance has been completed. Earlier searches were preliminary and found similar names in the wider market; no guarantee is made that Kicimondo or Kicipedia are legally available. Before public release, check exact and similar names in relevant territories, app stores, EUIPO/TMview, WIPO and relevant national registries. Consider classes 9, 28 and 41 as appropriate and seek professional advice if uncertain. Domain availability is a separate question.

No open-source license has been chosen for the project. Do not add a license or grant redistribution rights without the owner's decision. Third-party components retain their own licenses.
